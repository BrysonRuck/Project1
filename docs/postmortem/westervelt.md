## Biggest challenge
motivation to keep working. at some times i knew i had to do functionality, 
and i just really didn't want to. it's why my pr reports were kinda last minute; 
either my teammates hadn't closed my prs, or i was just finishing one that day. 
i realized i don't like the feeling of immense pressure and i like having things done 
rather than not, which made me work to get features/prs earlier towards the end of the project.

## Most valuable thing I learned
get the shit done early rather than late. when i started working to get my pr in on wednesday at the beginning, 
i was absolutely overwhelmed. on the last 2 weeks, i did prs on tuesday, and the sensation of being "free"
on wednesday made me feel a lot better than the first 2 weeks of the project. apart from human emotion,
it's good to have my stuff done early, in case there's issues linking it all together with everyone else's; there
definitely was issues with putting it all together and we were right down to the wire when turning it in, but we 
would've been turning in stuff late if i hadn't finished mystuff the night before.

## What I carry into Project 02
1. i will make prs by tuesdays - I will know it worked by checking project 2 and beyond on github.
2. i will delegate work to make my load less bearing - I will know it worked if i'm easily able to stick to commitment 1.
